## 3. What you're actually selling

You're not selling a website. **You're selling the recovery of patients they're already losing.**

Here's the pitch that works:

> "Dr. [Name], you're not losing patients because they want to book online. You're losing them because when they call at 8pm — or while you're mid-root-canal — nobody picks up, and they call the next clinic on Google. Zenora catches those patients three ways: they book a slot themselves, they leave a Priority callback request that's at the top of your dashboard in the morning, or they at minimum land on a site that looks like a clinic worth waiting for."

**Why this works:** It's honest. Dentists know most patients still call. If you say "everyone books online now," they'll call bullshit and you'll lose trust in the first 30 seconds. The missed-call angle is real, backed by data, and every dentist has experienced it.

### Quick competitor positioning

When a dentist brings up a competitor, use these:

| They mention | You say |
|---|---|
| **Practo** | "Practo puts you in a list next to twelve competitors. This is YOUR website, YOUR brand — nobody else's logo" |
| **Local agency** | "They build a brochure and disappear. You get a booking system, admin dashboard, and automated emails — and I still pick up in month six" |
| **Wix / WordPress** | "Who's building it? Your evenings are worth more than ₹1,500/month" |
| **"We're fine as-is"** | "It's not free. Every unanswered call is a patient at the clinic down the road" |

## 8. The demo — follow this exact sequence

**Duration: 15 minutes. Screen share or phone at their desk.**

⚠️ **Do NOT open the Analytics tab.** Fake data. If they click it: *"That's sample data — revenue tracking connects during setup."*

**Minute 0–2 — Their world**
> "Before I show you anything — how do patients book with you today? … And who handles the phone when you're with a patient?"

Let them talk. Write down the words they use. You'll repeat those words back later.

**Minute 2–5 — The patient's view**
Open the live site. Book an appointment as a patient, in front of them.
> "Patient searches, lands here, picks Root Canal, Thursday 11am, submits. Done. Fifteen seconds, 9pm, you were asleep."

Show the confirmation email arriving.
> "That went out automatically. Nobody at your clinic did anything."

**Minute 5–9 — Their view (THE MONEY MOMENT)**
Switch to the dashboard. The booking is sitting there.
> "That's the appointment you just watched get made."

Go to **Command Center**. Drag a card across.
> "This is today's patient flow. Waiting Room, Checkup, Treatment, Discharged. Your receptionist drags a card. Anyone can see where every patient is without shouting across the clinic."

**🔥 THIS IS THE MOMENT THEY BUY. Slow down. Let them ask for the mouse.**

**Minute 9–12 — Two features that pay for it**
1. **Priority Leads:** *"Patients who'd rather be called back land here, flagged. Your receptionist works this list first thing. These are the patients you're currently losing."*
2. **Visit-completed email:** *"When you mark Completed, they automatically get a thank-you with a Google review link. You said reviews matter — this runs forever."*

**Minute 12–15 — Price and close**
> "Setup is ₹44,999 — about one implant case. Then ₹2,499 a month, roughly ₹83 a day.
>
> Let me ask you honestly: if this brought you **one extra patient a month**, would that be worth ₹83 a day?"

Then **stop talking.** Whoever speaks first, loses.
