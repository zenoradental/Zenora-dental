## The three things that matter most

**1. Do not demo the Analytics tab.**
The revenue charts show hardcoded sample data, not real numbers. If a prospect clicks it on their own, say plainly: *"That's sample data — revenue tracking connects to your real pricing during setup."* Never present fake numbers as theirs.

**2. Never promise features that don't exist.**
SMS/WhatsApp reminders, online payments, insurance claims, X-ray imaging, Tally integration, multi-location, patient mobile app — **none of these are built.** If asked, say *"That's on the roadmap for next quarter."* Overpromising will kill your reputation in Bangalore's tight dental community.

**3. Walk-ins are your highest-converting channel.**
Your playbook has 5 mapped walk-in routes across Koramangala, Indiranagar, JP Nagar, Jayanagar, and Malleshwaram. Do 8–10 clinic visits per week. Leave the one-pager at reception. Follow up the next morning. This converts 3× better than cold email.
